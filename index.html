How this practice works

01 / PRIORITIZE & MIX

Weight the concepts, then pick a question

Within an eligible pool, a topic’s base weight is its question count raised to 1.35. The most recent topic gets a 0.2 multiplier to encourage interleaving. Then a question is picked within that topic, with extra weight for due misses and overdue reviews. This avoids multiplying frequency twice.

02 / SPACE THE REVIEWS

Five boxes, increasing intervals

A wrong answer enters Box 1, due in 1 minute. A first correct answer enters Box 2 (1 day), then advances to 3, 7, and 14 days. Correct answers in Box 5 stay on a 14-day interval. These are simple scheduling defaults, not a personalized memory model.

03 / RECALL BEFORE RECOGNITION

Work it out before you reveal

Active recall hides every answer choice. Write an optional draft or work on paper, then reveal and select your answer. Drafts are not automatically graded. “I couldn’t recall” counts as a miss and shows the worked solution. Choices keep the original exam letters.

When both due and unseen questions exist, the next draw chooses the due pool 75% of the time. A due miss receives 3× question weight; increasing lateness adds up to another 3×. Exact consecutive repeats are avoided when another eligible question exists. Extra practice preserves future dates after correct answers; misses still return in 1 minute.
